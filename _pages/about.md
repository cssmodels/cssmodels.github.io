<section class="home-hero">
  <p class="home-lead">
    Petter Törnberg researches how digital platforms and AI reshape public discourse, political conflict, urban life, and democratic institutions.
  </p>
  <p>
    His work brings computational social science into conversation with social theory. He is interested in how technologies become part of collective life: how they influence what people see, how groups form, how political conflict travels, and what new forms of power emerge.
  </p>
  <p>
    This site brings together his research, writing, projects, and conversations in the media.
  </p>
</section>
